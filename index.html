MIDI mapping & shortcuts

MK1/MK2: select Factory template 1. XL 3: use the DAW input. Connect MIDI activates its DAW mode. If you use a custom mode, MIDI Learn can pick up the MIDI messages it sends. In Keys mode, configure custom buttons to send momentary messages.

Upper MIDI buttons select tracks. Lower buttons edit eight steps or play notes in Keys mode. Track arrows move through the pattern eight steps at a time or shift the octave in Keys mode. Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes edits; Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes them. Space plays or stops. A S D F G H J K mirror the lower row. Your session and learned controls save in this browser.

Delete everything?

All notes and hits on every track will be deleted. Use Cmd/Ctrl+Z to undo.
